## 1. Role Overview & Core Expectations

Amazon's **Programmer Analyst Intern** role sits at the critical intersection of software development, systems integration, and business operations. Interns develop, integrate, troubleshoot, and evaluate large-scale internal tools and service-level applications that enable new business programs and global operational expansion.

### Key Mission & Day-to-Day Responsibilities:
- **Large-Scale & High-Availability Applications:** Design, build, and support tools that handle mission-critical internal workflows with high availability.
- **Service-Level Integrations & Migrations:** Perform service-to-service integrations, support platform migrations, and modify internal tools and service-level applications.
- **Process Automation & Bottleneck Elimination:** Identify performance and operational bottlenecks; automate manual configuration tasks and operational procedures.
- **Business-to-Technical Liaison:** Act as a bridge between business/operations teams and engineering development teams—eliciting, analyzing, communicating, and validating requirements for changes to software applications.
- **Amazon Tech Stack:** Hands-on utilization of AWS technologies (**AWS S3, DynamoDB, EC2**) alongside modern web services and schedulers.
- **Engineering Excellence & Mentorship:** Participate in daily scrums, write clean code and automated unit tests, debug application-level issues, and work with dedicated technical mentors and managers.
